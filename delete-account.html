PD Outlier · Updated November 16, 2026

Before you start

PD Outlier has no accounts, and we hold no data about you: your diary is only on your phone, plus any backup copies your phone's own iCloud or Google backup includes. Deleting it is permanent and cannot be undone.

Delete the diary on your phone

Delete the PD Outlier app from your phone. This removes your diary, its daily backup copies, your name and email if you gave them, and everything else the app stored.

Delete the copies in your phone's backup

If your phone's backup was turned on, it may still hold PD Outlier's backup copies after you delete the app.

  • iPhone: go to Settings → [your name] → iCloud → Manage Account Storage → Backups, choose your iPhone, and turn off PD Outlier. This deletes its data from your iCloud backup.
  • Android: the copies are part of your phone's Google backup. They are replaced the next time your phone backs up, and Google deletes a phone's backup if the phone isn't used for a while. To remove them sooner, delete your phone's backup in the Google One app or at drive.google.com → Storage → Backups.

Nothing to delete on our side

Because the app does not send your diary or anything you enter to us, there is nothing for us to delete, and no account to close.

Questions

Write to outlierpd@gmail.com. Our privacy policy explains what the app records and where it is kept.
