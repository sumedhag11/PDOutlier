Privacy Policy

PD Outlier · Effective November 16, 2026

The short version

  • Your diary stays on your phone. The app keeps everything in an encrypted database on your device, and nothing you enter is sent to us.
  • There is no account. We have no server holding your data, and the app makes no internet connections of its own.
  • Once a day the app saves a backup of your diary on your phone. If your phone's iCloud or Google backup is turned on, that backup is included in it, so your diary can come back on a new phone.
  • No ads, no tracking, and no analytics. We never sell your data.

Who this policy covers

This policy applies to the PD Outlier app for iPhone and Android, published by Sumedha Goel (“we”, “us”). If you have a question about it, write to outlierpd@gmail.com.

What the app records

Only what you enter yourself, plus a few details the app needs to work. All of it stays on your phone:

  • Your answers to the 21 items of the PRO-21 checklist each day, and the scores the app calculates from them
  • A history of changes to your answers, with the time of each change
  • Optional entries for how much you moved, how you feel, and a short written reflection
  • Your answers to the optional starting survey about your past month, if you take it
  • Your first name and email address, if you choose to give them. Both are optional, and neither is sent to us.
  • A random identifier the app creates on your phone, and the date you started using the app. The identifier is not linked to your name, your phone, or anything outside the app.

The app does not collect your location, contacts, photos, records from Apple Health or Google Health Connect, or your device's advertising identifier.

Where your diary is kept

Your diary is kept in an encrypted database on your phone. The key that unlocks it is held in your phone's secure storage (the Keychain on iPhone, the Keystore on Android). The database and its key are excluded from iCloud and Google backups.

The app does not send your diary, or anything else you enter, to us or to anyone else. Links in the app, such as the link to the published research, open in your phone's web browser.

Daily backup

Once a day, if anything has changed, the app saves a backup copy of your diary in its own storage on your phone, and keeps the three most recent copies. You can restore from one of them in Settings → Restore from backup.

These backup copies are the only part of the app's data included in your phone's own backup. If iCloud Backup (iPhone) or Google backup (Android) is turned on, your phone includes the backup copies in its backup, and Apple or Google stores them under their own privacy policies. This is what lets your diary come back when you set up a new phone from that backup. If your phone's backup is turned off, a lost or reset phone means a lost diary.

The backup copies are not encrypted by the app. Apple and Google encrypt the backups they store; with Advanced Data Protection turned on for iCloud, or on Android phones with a screen lock, those backups are end-to-end encrypted so only you can read them.

Health-related information

What you record describes food, movement, and how you feel, so we treat it as sensitive. It stays on your phone, apart from the backup copies your phone's own backup may include. It is never shared with advertisers or data brokers, and it is never used to profile you or to give you medical advice. The app is based on published research and is not a medical device.

Analytics, advertising, and tracking

The app carries no advertising, no analytics, no crash reporting, and no third-party tracking tools. We do not track you across other apps or websites, and we do not sell or share personal information.

How long things are kept

Your diary stays on your phone for as long as you keep the app installed. Deleting the app deletes your diary and its backup copies from your phone. Copies already included in your phone's iCloud or Google backup stay there until that backup is replaced or deleted; see how to delete your data.

Your choices

  • Every entry is optional, including the starting survey, your name, and your email address
  • Change or clear any answer on any day
  • Turn your phone's iCloud or Google backup on or off at any time, in your phone's settings
  • Delete everything by deleting the app — see how to delete your data

Because your diary stays on your phone, we hold no data about you, so there is nothing for us to access, correct, or delete on your behalf. Depending on where you live, you may have rights under laws such as the California Consumer Privacy Act or the General Data Protection Regulation; if you have a question about them, write to us.

Children

The app is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 13. The app stores nothing with us.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top of this page, and for any significant change we will tell you in the app before it takes effect. A future version may offer an optional account that syncs your diary; if it does, this policy will be updated first, and syncing will be your choice.
